NOVA is an AI-driven Cyber Risk Assessment platform designed specifically for small-to-medium businesses (SMBs). It bridges the gap for organizations that lack the budget for a dedicated security team and get overwhelmed by complex compliance frameworks like NIST or CIS. Instead of technical jargon, NOVA asks users plain-English questions about their daily workflows. It then translates those behavioral answers into a board-ready executive dashboard, complete with deterministic risk scoring and highly tailored, budget-friendly security recommendations.
